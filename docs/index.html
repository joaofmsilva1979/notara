WHAT YOU GET
Everything from one audio file
🎸
Guitar Tab
Full ASCII tab with fret positions, timing, and optional stereo L/R split for multi-guitar songs.
🎵
Bass Tab
4-string bass transcription from the isolated bass stem, exported as tab or MIDI.
🎤
Vocal Melody
Melody line transcribed in guitar octave notation — learn to play the vocal line on guitar.
🥁
Drum Summary
Groove map with kick, snare, hi-hat pattern and tempo detection.
🎹
Keys / MIDI
Keyboard part exported as MIDI — open directly in GarageBand, Ableton, or any DAW.
📄
Lyrics + Chords
Upload your lyrics and get chord names positioned above the right words. Export as PDF or TXT.
HOW IT WORKS
Three steps
1
Drop your audio
Upload an MP3, WAV, M4A, FLAC, or OGG file — or paste a YouTube URL.
2
Hit Transcribe
Notara separates the stems, detects chords, maps notes to the fretboard. Takes 1–5 minutes.
3
Download your tabs
Export as PDF, TXT, or MIDI. Play back with real-time chord highlighting.

Download Notara

Runs entirely on your computer. No internet required after install.
Needs Python 3.9+ and ~2 GB RAM.

10.15 Catalina or newer

cd notara && ./install.sh && ./start.sh
