— Why

Sometimes you need to work with your ears. Sometimes you need a little help. There's no shame in the second one — every great guitarist learns from transcribing, and transcribing takes time that not everyone has. I made Notara because I wanted that help to exist, and to be honest about what it is: a starting point, not a finished score.

— How

Notara runs Demucs to separate the guitar and bass stems from your audio, then uses basic-pitch to transcribe notes, detects the key and chords, and maps everything to a real fretboard — accounting for tuning, position, and whether there's one guitar or two. The output is an actual tab you can play from. Built in Python. Shaped with Claude.

— What

Guitar tab. Bass tab. PDF. MIDI. For any tuning — standard, drop, open, 7-string, 8-string. Auto-detected or manually set. Free. Open source. github.com/joaofmsilva1979/notara ↗

🔒

Nothing is saved. Your audio is processed and immediately discarded. No account, no tracking, no storage. What you upload stays between you and the algorithm.

Notara is free. No catch, no account, no ads.
